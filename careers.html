Why Atélier

Healthcare has grown more sophisticated, but the experience of receiving it hasn’t kept pace. Care is fragmented, clients are left to connect the dots, and treatment rarely looks past what hurts today.

Atélier Health brings complementary disciplines together under one roof, with one client record and one plan. Better care starts with better information, a connected team and real investment in the person behind every appointment.

We aren’t building another clinic. We’re building a preventative model of care, and we want the clinicians who shape it from day one: how care is delivered, the standards we hold and the culture we keep.

How care works here

The whole client, not just the complaint.

The best outcomes happen when clinicians have the autonomy to practise their discipline, the chance to learn from each other and the information to make better decisions.

  1. UnderstandEvery new client starts with our Foundational Health Protocol: a meaningful health baseline, the areas to improve and an individual roadmap.
  2. HealYou treat what’s in front of you, in your discipline, on your own clinical judgment, with the full picture in hand.
  3. LiveCare continues past today’s complaint, toward long-term health and longevity, with a clear plan and a reason to return.

Engagement & compensation

How we work together.

All positions are independent contractor engagements, not employment. Terms are set with each clinician and documented in a written agreement.

  • Commission-basedAn agreed percentage of collected treatment revenue. The split is set with you individually.
  • Health spending accountAn annual reimbursement allowance for eligible healthcare expenses you incur personally.
  • Potential equityOpportunities for eligible clinicians to share in Atélier’s long-term ownership and growth, under separate agreements.
  • Personal brandingOur Creative + Marketing Director works with you to build your own online presence, so you attract clients organically while you practise with us and after.
  • Clinic infrastructureDesignated treatment space, equipment, standard supplies, booking and payment systems, and administrative support.

Who we’re looking for

More than a series of appointments.

  • You’re curious about the bigger picture, and you value education, prevention and collaboration.
  • You take pride in your clinical work, value the relationships you build with clients and know there’s always more to learn.
  • You want to work alongside other disciplines, share what you know and lift the standard of care for everyone.
  • Above all, you’d rather help shape the future of healthcare than keep working inside the system as it is.

Requirements

  • Current registration in good standing with your regulatory college in British Columbia
  • Professional liability insurance
  • Strong clinical reasoning and a commitment to evidence-informed practice
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • A commitment to client education, individualized care and working across disciplines
  • Comfortable working independently within a collaborative team

Experience in integrated or multidisciplinary practice, performance and recovery, or preventative health and longevity is an asset. So is an existing client base, though it isn’t required.
